How teams and the weekly count work

A CountAble team is you and your buddies, up to 20 people. Each of you picks your own activity and weekly number. The app counts down what each person has left, and every Sunday the week closes with a board of who hit their number.

Updated October 25, 2026

1. Start a team

On the Basic or Premium plan, tap ADD TEAM on DATA, or START A TEAM on COUNT. The app first asks how your team will talk.

2. Pick how your team talks

There are three ways. The team's creator picks one and can switch later under HOW YOUR TEAM TALKS on the team's card.

TEAM TEXT IN APP

Your team chats inside CountAble, with notifications, and your coach joins in: it answers when someone asks it something, and posts the Sunday board. Messages are kept for 90 days.

AUTO-TEXT BY CREATOR

Rather skip notifications? Stay in your own group text. The creator's iPhone posts the Sunday board there by itself, through a Shortcut. Needs iOS 27 or later.

COPY & PASTE BY CREATOR

Rather skip notifications, or an Android phone in the group? Each Sunday the creator gets the week's board in the app to copy and paste into your group text.

The coach is never added to a group text.

3. Invite your buddies

INVITE A BUDDY makes a one-time code for one friend. It is good for 14 days, and you send it from your own Messages: CountAble never texts your friends. They install CountAble on their iPhone and enter the code under JOIN A TEAM. A friend who accepts gets Basic free for 30 days, once per person.

4. Everyone picks their own number

  • A countable activity and a weekly number. Push-ups, miles, classes: whatever you count. You can count more than one, and your week is a hit when you reach every one of them.
  • Or a goal by a date. Set a big total and a last day, such as 10,000 push-ups by December 31. The app works out your weekly number and re-paces it each week from what you have banked. Your goal is your own: a buddy can have a different one, or none.
  • Or let your watch count it. An activity can be filled in from Apple Health instead of typed. It can count six things: miles run, miles biked, miles walked, steps, workout minutes, and minutes in a heart-rate zone.

5. Count through the week

Your week lives on PLAN as day cards. Swipe a day right when it is done and left when it is missed. A day linked to your activity counts when you mark it done. Your COUNT card always shows what is left this week; double-tap it to flip to the team's scoreboard.

Watch numbers come from Apple Health on your phone, so they arrive when you open the app. If a number looks low, open CountAble.

6. Sunday: the board

Every Sunday, a team with at least two people gets the week's board:

  • DONE: everyone who hit their weekly number, with their count.
  • FELL SHORT: everyone who did not, with theirs.
  • TEAM HOT STREAK: how many weeks in a row everyone hit.

Then come a couple of short lines: who came up short, and one line to get the team going. A fresh week starts after that.

7. A wager, if your team wants one

  • The Wager: a weekly wager between buddies. Hit your numbers, or make good on whatever the team agreed.
  • Solo Mission: no buddy yet? Miss a week and you honor a pledge to a cause you name. It becomes a wager between you when a friend joins.

Each person is judged only on their own numbers. CountAble keeps the board and never holds or moves money.

The limits

  • Up to 20 people on a team, and up to 5 teams per person.
  • One team's data is never shown to another team.
  • Everyone on a team has the same experience inside it. The plan only decides who can be on it.
  • iPhone only, with a US or Canadian mobile number.
