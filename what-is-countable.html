What is CountAble?

CountAble is an iPhone app for workout accountability with friends. You start a team and each person picks their own weekly number: 100 push-ups, 15 miles, three classes. Everyone on the team can see who has done their number and who still has some left. Every Sunday the week closes and a fresh one starts.

Updated October 25, 2026 · Coming soon to the iOS App Store.

The short version

  • Teams of up to 20 people. Each person counts their own thing, so runners and lifters can share a team.
  • A weekly number, not a someday goal. Your card shows what is left this week and drops as you go.
  • One scoreboard. Every Sunday the team sees who is DONE, who FELL SHORT, and how many weeks in a row everyone hit.
  • An optional weekly wager between buddies. CountAble keeps the board and never holds or moves money.
  • Apple Health. Workouts your Apple Watch records can fill in your count. CountAble only reads Apple Health and never writes to it.
  • A coach on Premium. An AI coach drafts each week's plan around you. Nothing is saved until you approve it.
  • Three plans. Free, Basic at $3.99 a month (teams), and Premium at $4.99 to $12.99 a month (your own coach). A friend who accepts your team invite gets Basic free for 30 days.

The three tabs

COUNT

A card for each weekly activity, showing what remains this week. Double-tap a card to flip it to your team's scoreboard. Event countdowns live here too.

PLAN

Your week as day cards. Swipe a day right when it is done, left when it is missed. Press and hold a marked day to unlock it.

DATA

Your teams and buddies, events, the coach, the WATCH & LEARN films, and your account.

How a week works

  1. Each person picks a countable activity and a weekly number.
  2. Through the week you mark days done on PLAN, or let Apple Health bring in your watch workouts. The number on your COUNT card drops as you go.
  3. On Sunday the week closes. Your team's board shows who hit their number, who fell short, and the TEAM HOT STREAK (weeks in a row everyone hit).
  4. A fresh week starts, with every number back to full.

How teams and the weekly count work, step by step

The coach (Premium)

Premium adds a private AI coach called THE COACHINATOR. It builds weekly plans around you and rewrites them when you ask. It holds a Sunday conversation that shapes your next week, and it sends reminders at the times you pick. Every plan it writes stays a draft until you approve it.

The coach is an AI, not a doctor, and nothing it says is medical advice. If a message sounds like an emergency, it stops coaching and points you to 911 or 988.

What CountAble is not

  • It is not a money app. A wager is a line on your team's board, and your team makes good on it themselves.
  • It is not medical advice, and it is not a medical device.
  • It is not on Android yet. CountAble is for iPhone.

What you need

  • An iPhone.
  • A US or Canadian mobile number. You sign in with a 6-digit code texted to it. There is no password.
  • For a team: the Basic plan, or a friend's team invite (Basic free for 30 days, once per person).
