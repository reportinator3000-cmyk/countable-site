CountAble SMS Program

CountAble, from CountAble LLC, texts your mobile number for one reason: signing in. Your coach, your reminders and your team reach you inside the app, never by text. This page describes the texts we send.

What we text

  • A one-time 6-digit sign-in code, only when you ask for one in the app
  • The same kind of code when you add or change your number in the app
  • A one-time notice if your number is later confirmed on a different CountAble account
  • A reply when you text our number: HELP, STOP and START are answered, and any other text from a CountAble member gets one line pointing them to the app, at most once a day. If a text to us suggests you may be in danger, we reply with crisis resources.

We never send coaching, reminders, team updates or marketing by text.

Signing in

A mobile number is the only way to sign in to CountAble. On the app's sign-in screen you type your own number and tap "TEXT ME A CODE". One 6-digit code is texted only when you tap that button, and it expires in 10 minutes. Under the code boxes the screen says "By entering your code you agree to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy." Entering the code signs you in and proves the number is yours. Asking for a code does not sign you up for any other text.

How often

Only when you ask: one text for each code you request, and no more than six codes to one number in a day. The notice about a moved number is sent once. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Stopping texts and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time and we stop texting your number. While it is stopped we cannot text you a sign-in code.
  • Reply START to get sign-in codes again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email support@getcountable.app.

Privacy

Your phone number is used only to sign you in and to keep your account secure. We do not sell personal data, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
