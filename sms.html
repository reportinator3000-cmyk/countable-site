CountAble SMS Program

CountAble texts a one-time sign-in code only when you ask for one, and sends recurring messages only to users (and accountability buddies) who explicitly opt in. This page describes the program.

What messages we send

  • One-time sign-in and phone verification codes, sent only when you ask for one
  • Workout reminders you schedule yourself in the app
  • Weekly progress summaries and team recaps
  • Accountability-buddy invitations and updates (sent only after the recipient consents)
  • Occasional messages from your coach about CountAble's paid plans and features

Signing in

A mobile number is how you sign in to CountAble. On the app's sign-in screen you enter your own number and tap "TEXT ME A CODE". One 6-digit code is texted only when you tap that button; entering it signs you in and proves the number is yours. Asking for a code does not opt you in to any other message.

How opt-in works

After the number is verified, the app shows the consent notice below, and the user must explicitly accept it. No one receives recurring messages without completing this step.

"By opting in, you agree to receive recurring workout reminders, accountability messages, coach texts, and occasional messages about CountAble plans and features. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. You can change reminder settings in the app."

Message frequency

Frequency varies with your settings — typically up to a few messages per day (your own scheduled reminders) plus weekly summaries. Messages about paid plans are limited to a few in your first days and stop on their own. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages immediately.
  • Reply START to resume messages after stopping.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact support@getcountable.app.
  • You can also pause SMS anytime from the app's settings.

Privacy

Phone numbers and SMS consent records are used solely to operate these features. We do not sell personal data, and no mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy.
