Support

If you need help with CountAble, we’re here to assist.

Common Topics

  • Signing in (a code texted to your mobile number)
  • Workout tracking issues
  • Notifications and how often they come (DATA → NOTIFICATIONS in the app)
  • Texts (sign-in codes only) — see the SMS Program page
  • Account deletion

Contact Us

Email: support@getcountable.app

Please include:

  • The mobile number on your account
  • A brief description of the issue
  • Screenshots (if helpful)

Response Time

We aim to respond within 24–48 hours on business days.

Account & Data Requests

For account deletion or data-related inquiries, you may:

  • Use in-app tools (DATA tab → ACCOUNT)
  • Contact support directly
