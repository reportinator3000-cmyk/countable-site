Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

CountAble (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy describes what the app actually collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. We have tried to write it plainly rather than defensively.

The short version

  • We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.
  • Your Apple Health data is used to coach you and nothing else.
  • Your coach is an AI. What you tell it, and the workout and health figures it reasons about, are sent to our AI provider to write each reply.
  • The operator of CountAble can see your account details and your conversations with the coach, to run and improve the service.
  • If your team uses TEAM TEXT IN APP, your teammates see what you write there, and the coach reads it when someone asks it something.
  • If you invite a friend, we store the name and number you picked for them.
  • If you invite your coach to a calendar event, we read that event so it can appear on your plan. We never connect to your calendar account.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Mobile phone number (it is how you sign in)
  • Email address (to confirm your account, and so your coach can answer calendar invitations you send it)
  • Authentication data
  • Your name and time zone, as you enter them
  • App preferences and settings

Workout and Coaching Data

  • Workout plans, activities, goals, and completion history
  • Answers you give during athlete intake
  • Messages between you and your coach, by text or in the app, kept as one conversation so the coach remembers your history
  • Notes the coach records about your training in order to coach you consistently

Apple Health Data (only if you allow it)

If you grant permission, we read a limited set of data from Apple Health:

  • Workouts (type, duration, and distance)
  • Active energy burned and exercise minutes
  • Heart rate for a workout, including average, maximum, heart-rate zones, and splits
  • Resting heart rate and heart rate variability, read daily to judge recovery against your own baseline
  • Sleep: minutes asleep per night (time in bed is not counted)
  • Your date of birth, only to estimate your maximum heart rate for heart-rate zones
  • Blood glucose, only if you turn on the daily glucose text — your coach does not interpret it

We read a rolling recent window, not your entire history, and we never write anything back to Apple Health. Daily totals and per-workout summaries are stored on our servers so your coach and your plan can use them. You can revoke this at any time in the iOS Health app; we keep what was already collected until you delete your account or ask us to remove it.

Calendar Events (only the ones you send us)

Your coach has an email address of its own. Nothing happens unless you use it: we have no connection to your calendar account, no access to your calendar, and no way to look at anything you have not sent. If you add that address as a guest on one of your own calendar events, your calendar emails us that single invitation, and we read it so the workout can appear on your plan.

From an invitation you send us, we store:

  • The event's title, date, time, and length
  • Its location and description, if you filled them in
  • The organiser's email address, and the identifier your calendar uses for the event, so that later changes update the same entry instead of creating a duplicate

Your coach replies to the invitation as an accepted guest, so you can see it arrived. If you cancel the event, we remove the plan entry it created. Bear in mind that everyone else invited to that event can see your coach's address on the guest list — so treat it as you would any other guest, and do not add it to events you would not want it to read.

Text Messages (only if you turn on texting)

  • Your consent status
  • The content of texts between you and your coach
  • A delivery record for each message we send, which stores the message length and a scrambled (hashed) form of the number plus its last four digits — never the full number and never the message text

Team Chat (only on teams that use TEAM TEXT IN APP)

The messages you write in your team's chat are stored for 90 days and shown to your team; someone who joins later sees messages from when they joined. When someone asks the coach something, recent messages are sent to our AI provider so the coach can answer. If a message is reported, its words are kept with the report so it can be reviewed, and cleared within 90 days of the review.

Notifications (only if you turn them on)

We keep your phone's notification address until you turn them off or sign out. Notifications are delivered through Apple and our notification provider, and show the team's name and who wrote, never the words.

Contacts (only the one you pick)

When you invite someone, iOS shows you its own contact picker and hands us only the single contact you choose. We do not read, upload, or store your address book. If that picker is unavailable, the app may ask for contacts permission to show a list inside the app instead; that list stays on your device and is never uploaded.

If you tap to save your coach as a contact, the app adds one contact card — your coach's name and the number it texts from — to your address book. Nothing is read from your address book to do it.

Device, Diagnostic, and Usage Information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Crash and error reports, with phone numbers, email addresses, and access tokens stripped out before they leave your device
  • Product usage events (for example: signed up, generated a plan, completed a workout), recorded against a scrambled (hashed) version of your account id rather than your identity

2. Information About People Who Are Not Users

This one deserves saying out loud. When you invite a friend, we store the name and phone number you selected for them so the invite can be sent, so your team roster can show who is pending, and so you can resend it. That person has not agreed to anything with us at that point.

  • We use their details only to deliver and track that invitation.
  • We do not build a profile for them, market to them, or share their details with anyone else.
  • Revoking an invite in the app removes the stored details.
  • If someone wants their information removed, they or you can email us and we will delete it.

Please only invite people who would welcome the text. You are the one who knows that.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To run the app and keep your data in sync across sessions
  • To generate your plans, coaching replies, recaps, and reminders
  • To send the reminders and messages you have asked for
  • To keep the service working: diagnosing errors, preventing abuse, and enforcing limits

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use your Apple Health data for anything except coaching you.

4. Your Coach Is an AI

CountAble's coaching is generated by an artificial-intelligence model run by a third-party provider. To write a reply, a plan, or a recap, we send that provider the relevant context — which can include your first name, your goals and intake answers, your workout history and how consistent you have been, your recent messages with the coach, and, where relevant to the feature, figures derived from your Apple Health data such as heart rate and training load.

  • The provider processes this to produce the response and returns it to us.
  • We do not permit your data to be used for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • The coach can be wrong. It is not a doctor, and it does not give medical advice.
  • If you would rather the coach not see your Apple Health data, do not grant Health permission — the rest of the app still works.

5. Text Messages and Group Threads

If you opt in to texting:

  • You consent to receive messages about your activity and accountability
  • Message frequency varies; standard message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help; you can also turn texting off in the app
  • Your number is shared with our messaging provider in order to deliver the messages

If your team posts its weekly board into its own group text (AUTO-TEXT BY CREATOR or TEXT FORWARD BY CREATOR), that group text is yours, not ours: we are not in it, and everyone in it sees each other's numbers as in any group text. With AUTO-TEXT, the team's creator holds a private link their iPhone uses to fetch the weekly board (first names and counts).

6. Who Can See Your Activity and Conversations

Your teammates

Teams exist to make consistency visible, so some of your activity is visible to others by design:

  • Your team's owner can see each member's display name and how consistently they hit their commitment
  • Other members see their own figures, plus what appears in shared recaps and the team chat
  • Your private one-to-one conversations with your coach are never shown to your team
  • One team's information is never shown to another team

Joining a team is always your choice: you join by entering an invite code yourself. Nobody can add you to a team without that.

The operator of CountAble

The operator of CountAble can see your account details and your conversations with the coach, to run and improve the service.

The operator can also see the notes your coach keeps about your training, and a record of what the coach was shown and what it wrote when it built or rewrote a plan for you. The sections of those notes and records that hold your Apple Health and watch figures are left out.

The operator does not read team chats, except a message someone reports.

7. Safety Screening

Incoming messages are automatically screened for language suggesting a crisis. If that screen triggers, the app sends support resources and quiets the coach's usual personality for a while. This is automated, the message text is not written into our operational logs by the screen, and it is not a substitute for emergency help. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services.

8. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Our hosting and database provider, which stores the app's data
  • Our messaging provider, which delivers your texts and therefore receives your phone number and message content
  • Our AI provider, which receives the coaching context described in section 4
  • Apple and our notification provider, which deliver lock-screen notifications if you turn them on
  • Our crash-reporting provider, which receives error reports after sensitive values are stripped out
  • Apple, and our purchase provider, for subscriptions — we never see your payment details
  • Legal authorities, where we are required by law

Media attached to team recaps is delivered through temporary links that expire shortly after they are created. Once a message reaches your phone, your carrier and your device hold their own copy, on their own terms.

9. Analytics, Diagnostics, and Your Opt-Out

Product analytics and error reports are recorded against a scrambled version of your account id. You can switch product analytics off under Account in the app.

Turning analytics off does not switch off the records we need to run the service safely: security and authentication records, backend error logs, message delivery records, and abuse-prevention counters.

A small, named group of operators can read aggregated diagnostic data in order to debug problems.

10. Data Retention

  • Your account data is kept while your account exists
  • Team chat messages, and the Sunday card posted in a team chat, are deleted after 90 days; other media attached to team recaps after 60 days
  • Diagnostic and analytics records are kept only as long as they are useful for debugging and abuse prevention, and are not tied to your identity
  • Message delivery records are kept as proof that opt-outs were honored and to resolve delivery disputes; they contain no message text and no full phone numbers

11. Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account at any time under Account in the app. It happens immediately, and it is not reversible.

What deletion does:

  • Removes your profile, workouts, plans, goals, coach conversations, coach notes, Apple Health data we stored, calendar invitations you sent us, phone number, team memberships, and your team chat messages, alert settings and notification address
  • Removes you from any live group text thread, and deletes threads belonging to teams you own
  • Removes invitations you created, including the invitee details in them

What deliberately survives, and why:

  • Message delivery records, which hold no message text and no full numbers — kept as evidence that opt-out requests were honored
  • Diagnostic and analytics records that were already stored against a scrambled id rather than your identity
  • A dated record that a deletion occurred, identified only by a scrambled id

Backups held by our infrastructure providers age out on their own retention schedules after deletion from our active systems.

12. Data Security

Your login is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. Traffic to our servers uses encrypted connections. On the server, access rules are enforced per-account in the database itself, so one account cannot read another's data, and your subscription status and identity are determined server-side rather than trusted from the app. Verification codes are stored only in hashed form.

No system is completely secure, and we will not pretend otherwise.

13. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your data. The app offers deletion directly under Account, and you can email us for anything else.

14. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for anyone under 13, or the applicable minimum age in your region, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. If a change materially affects what we collect or who receives it, we will make that clear in the app rather than quietly changing the date.

16. Contact

Questions, or a request to remove someone's details: support@getcountable.app
