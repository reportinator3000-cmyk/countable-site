What is CountAble?

An iPhone app for workout accountability with friends. Each person on a team picks a weekly number, everyone can see who has done theirs, and the week resets every Sunday. More about CountAble

Is CountAble free?

There is a Free plan: plan your week by hand and log your events and counts, on your own. Teams need Basic at $3.99 a month, and a friend who accepts your team invite gets Basic free for 30 days, once per person. Premium, $4.99 to $12.99 a month by the coach brain you pick, adds your own AI coach. Both paid plans are monthly; there is no yearly plan. Plans and prices

Can I download it now?

CountAble is coming soon to the iOS App Store. It is in testing now.

Do my friends need the app?

Yes. Each buddy installs CountAble on their own iPhone and joins your team with the one-time code you send them from your own Messages. The code is good for 14 days.

Can my friends and I do different workouts?

Yes. Each person picks their own activity and weekly number. One buddy can count miles while another counts push-ups.

How many people can be on a team?

Up to 20. One person can be on up to 5 teams.

What happens on Sunday?

The week closes. Your team gets a board of who is DONE, who FELL SHORT, and the TEAM HOT STREAK (weeks in a row everyone hit), and a fresh week starts. How teams work

Does CountAble handle money for wagers?

No. A wager is a line on your team's board. CountAble never holds or moves money, and your team makes good on what it agreed by itself.

Does CountAble work with Apple Watch?

Through Apple Health. CountAble reads the workouts your Apple Watch (or any app that writes to Apple Health) records, such as time, distance and heart rate. A weekly activity can be counted from the watch: miles run, miles biked, miles walked, steps, workout minutes, or minutes in a heart-rate zone. CountAble never writes to Apple Health.

Is the coach a real person?

No. The coach on Premium, THE COACHINATOR, is an AI. It drafts weekly plans around you, and a plan is saved only when you approve it. It is not a doctor and nothing it says is medical advice. If a message sounds like an emergency, it stops coaching and points you to 911 or 988.

How do I sign in?

With your mobile number. Type it in, tap TEXT ME A CODE, and enter the 6-digit code. There is no password and no email sign-in. US and Canadian mobile numbers only.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet. CountAble is for iPhone.

Does CountAble sell my data?

No. CountAble does not sell your data and does not use it for advertising. One team's data is never shown to another team. Privacy Policy
